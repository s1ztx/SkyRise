Guidelines

Server Rules

Breaking these rules results in warnings, mutes, or permanent bans. We take fair play seriously.

01
Respect All Players
Treat every player with basic respect. Disrespectful behavior, insults, or put-downs won't be tolerated.
02
No Cheating or Hacking
Any form of hacked client, cheat software, or game exploit is a permanent ban offense — no exceptions.
03
No Griefing
Destroying or stealing from other players' builds is forbidden in all non-PvP zones.
04
No Spamming
Flooding chat with repeated messages, symbols, or commands disrupts others and is a mutable offense.
05
No Advertising
Promoting other servers, Discord servers, or external services in chat is strictly prohibited.
06
Follow Staff Instructions
When a staff member issues a directive, comply immediately. Disputes can be raised through our ticket system.
07
No Inappropriate Content
Mature, offensive, or NSFW content in any form — chat, builds, skins — results in immediate action.
08
No Exploiting Bugs
Report bugs through proper channels. Exploiting them for personal gain is cheating and treated as such.
09
No Toxicity or Harassment
Targeted harassment, hate speech, or discrimination based on any identity is a permanent ban offense.
10
English Only in Global Chat
Use English in the global channel so staff can moderate effectively. Language-specific channels available on Discord.

Frequently Asked Questions
What version of Minecraft do you support?
We support Java Edition 1.8–1.21 and Bedrock Edition. Java players get the most complete experience, but Bedrock players can join via our cross-play gateway.
Is the server free to play?
Absolutely. You can enjoy the full server experience with no paywalls. Premium ranks offer cosmetic and quality-of-life bonuses but never pay-to-win advantages.
How do I report a player or bug?
Use /report [username] in-game, or open a ticket in our Discord server. For bug reports, use the #bug-reports channel with steps to reproduce.
Can I apply to become staff?
Yes! Applications open periodically in our Discord. You must be at least 14, have 100+ hours on the server, and maintain a clean record to be eligible.
How do rank purchases work?
Ranks are purchased through our store and delivered automatically within 5 minutes. Subscriptions auto-renew monthly unless cancelled before the renewal date.
Are there refund policies?
Refunds are offered within 24 hours of purchase if the rank was not used. After use, no refunds are issued per our store policy. Contact support for exceptions.
