Server Rules

Know The Code

These rules apply to all players on all game modes. Ignorance of the rules is not a valid defense. Last updated: December 2024.

Jump To Section

By connecting to play.skyrise.net, you agree to abide by these rules. Staff have final say in all moderation decisions. Rules may be updated without prior notice — it is your responsibility to check this page periodically. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask a staff member before doing it.
§ 01
General Conduct
Warning
Respect all players and staff
Treat every person on the server the way you would want to be treated. This means no belittling, name-calling, or sustained negative behavior directed at any individual.
Temp Ban
No harassment or targeted behaviour
Repeatedly following, camping spawns near, or otherwise harassing a specific player outside of agreed PvP zones is prohibited. A single warning will be issued before a temporary ban is applied.
Permanent Ban
No threats, doxxing, or real-life harm
Threatening to harm anyone in real life, revealing a player's personal information, or attempting to DDoS the server or any player results in an immediate permanent ban with no appeal window for 90 days.
Warning
Impersonating staff
You may not use a username, nickname, or skin that is designed to look like or be confused with a SkyRise staff member. Staff are listed on the Staff page.
§ 02
Chat Rules
Mute
No spam or excessive caps
Sending the same message repeatedly, flooding chat with symbols, or using ALL CAPS for more than a single word will result in a mute. Mutes escalate: 1 hour → 24 hours → 7 days.
Temp Mute/Ban
No hate speech or slurs
Any form of hate speech based on race, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, or disability is not tolerated. A single occurrence results in an immediate 7-day mute. Repeat offenders are permanently banned.
Warning
Keep chat in English on global channels
Global and trade chat must be in English so staff can moderate effectively. You are welcome to speak other languages in private messages or faction/island chats.
Warning
No advertising other servers
Posting IPs or names of other Minecraft servers anywhere on SkyRise — chat, signs, books, Discord — will result in an immediate mute. Repeat offenses escalate to a ban.
§ 03
Gameplay Rules
Permanent Ban
No exploiting bugs or duplication glitches
If you discover a bug that provides an unfair advantage — including item duplication, unlimited money, or bypassing game mechanics — you must report it immediately at discord.gg/MyGvcFNYd. Exploiting bugs is a permanent ban offense. Reporters are rewarded.
Temp Ban
No AFK farms that strain the server
Automated AFK machines that repeatedly trigger entity spawning, mob grinding, or redstone at rates above 20 activations per second are prohibited. Staff will ask you to remove the machine before issuing a ban.
Warning
No schematica printer mode
Schematica's printer feature automatically places blocks and provides an unfair building advantage. Schematica may be used in display mode only. Printer mode will be treated as a macro/auto-clicker offense.
§ 04
Cheating & Hacking
Permanent Ban
No hacked clients or cheat mods
Any client modification that gives an in-game advantage — kill aura, speed hacks, fly hacks, xray, ESP, auto-sprint, reach hacks, and similar — results in an immediate permanent ban. No appeal is accepted within 60 days of the ban date.
Temp Ban
No auto-clickers or macros in PvP
Hardware or software auto-clickers and macros that automate clicking are banned during PvP. CPS above 20 recorded by our anti-cheat triggers an automatic 3-day ban pending staff review.
Warning
Allowed mods
Optifine, Sodium, Iris, Litematica (no print), Inventory Tweaks, and minimap mods without player radar are permitted. If your mod is not listed and you are unsure, ask staff before using it.
§ 05
Griefing & Stealing
Rollback + Ban
No griefing in protected zones
Griefing player builds in protected claims, islands, or towns results in a full rollback of all damage plus a 14-day ban for a first offense. Second offense is permanent.
Temp Ban
No stealing from unlocked chests
Taking items from an unlocked chest that does not belong to you is still stealing. Always ask before taking. "It was unlocked" is not a defense on SkyRise.
Warning
Wilderness griefing policy
Destroying terrain in the wilderness to reach ores is allowed, but leaving floating trees, large craters, or lava pits near spawn roads is prohibited. Clean up after yourself or staff will issue a warning and charge a fee in in-game currency.
§ 06
Builds & Terrain
Build Removal + Ban
No offensive or inappropriate builds
Builds depicting sexual content, hate symbols, real-world violence, or content intended to shock or offend will be removed without notice and the builder banned. Staff judgment is final.
Warning
No claim blocking or land camping
Surrounding another player's claim with your own claims to prevent them from expanding — known as claim blocking — is prohibited. Land must be claimed in good faith for active use.
§ 07
PvP Rules
Warning
No spawn killing
Killing a player within 10 seconds of them respawning or teleporting to a safe zone is spawn killing. First offense is a warning; repeated offenses escalate to a temp ban.
Temp Ban
No combat logging
Disconnecting while in active combat to avoid dying is combat logging. Our system will kill your character after 15 seconds. Repeatedly logging to avoid death after receiving a warning results in a ban.
§ 08
Discord Rules
Warning
Use the correct channels
Post in appropriate channels. Support questions go in #open-ticket, suggestions go in #suggestions, screenshots go in #media. Off-topic conversation belongs in #general.
Discord Ban
No NSFW content anywhere
Posting NSFW images, videos, or links in any channel — including DMs to staff — results in a permanent Discord server ban and an in-game ban.
Warning
No unsolicited DMs advertising
DMing members to advertise services, trades, other servers, or external websites is prohibited and will result in a Discord ban.

§ 10
Appealing a Ban
How to appeal
Join our Discord at discord.gg/MyGvcFNYd and open a ticket in #open-ticket using the Appeal template. Include your in-game name, the date of the ban, the reason shown, and your explanation. Appeals are reviewed within 72 hours. Appeals for hacking offenses are reviewed no sooner than 60 days after the ban date.
